What size does it print?

US Letter (8.5 × 11"), designed to print at home on a standard printer. It also prints cleanly on A4 — choose "fit to page" in your print dialog. Print the whole binder once, then reprint just the trackers and logs whenever you need fresh ones.

Is this medical advice?

No. CareNest is an organising tool. The binder helps you keep the information your care team gives you in one place and share it clearly — it never recommends treatments or medications. Always follow your own doctor's guidance, and in an emergency call your local emergency number first.
