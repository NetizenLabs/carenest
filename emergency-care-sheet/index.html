Questions

The honest answers.

Is it really free?

Yes. Both pages, no card, no trial. CareNest makes money later from the full Caregiver Binder — the free sheet is how you decide whether our system fits your life before you spend anything.

What size does it print?

US Letter (8.5 × 11"). It also scales cleanly to A4 — choose "fit to page" in your print dialog.

Is this medical advice?

No. CareNest is an organising tool. It helps you keep information in one place and share it clearly — it never recommends treatments or medications. Always follow your own doctor's guidance.

Who is CareNest for?

Family caregivers — mostly daughters, sons and spouses — caring for an aging parent, often on their own. If you are the person everyone calls, this was made for you.
